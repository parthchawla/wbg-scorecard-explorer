# How to use the Scorecard assistant

Ask questions in plain language, in any language. The assistant looks up the answer in the World Bank Group's
official Scorecard data and explains what it found. Every number it gives comes from that data.

## What you can ask

| You want to know… | Try asking |
|---|---|
| **What the WBG has delivered** | "How many people has the World Bank Group helped get electricity?" |
| **Where results are happening** | "Which regions reached the most people with broadband internet?" |
| **Progress over time** | "How have health results changed since FY2024?" |
| **Fragile, poor or small countries** | "Compare climate resilience results in fragile states with the global total." |
| **A specific country** | "What has the World Bank Group achieved in Kenya?" |
| **Need vs delivery** | "Which regions have the lowest electricity access, and how many people are WBG projects reaching there?" |
| **Concrete projects** | "Which projects are bringing clean water to Ethiopia?" |
| **Women and youth** | "How many girls and women have been supported with better education?" |
| **What an indicator means** | "What counts as 'private capital mobilized'?" |

**Tips**
- Name a **topic** (electricity, schools, jobs, climate…) and, if you like, a **place** (a country, region, "fragile states", "low-income countries").
- Say **"since FY2024"** or **"over time"** to see change across years.
- Ask follow-ups: "Show that as a chart", "Only IFC", "What about women?", "Which projects?"

## What data it searches

The **WBG Corporate Scorecard** (scorecard.worldbank.org) is the World Bank Group's official report on its results. The assistant uses three kinds of figures from it:

| Kind | What it shows | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **WBG Results**: the main focus | What projects financed by the World Bank Group (IBRD, IDA, IFC, MIGA) have delivered | 258 million people provided with electricity access |
| **Client Context** | Conditions in the countries the WBG works with. Shaped by everyone, not just the WBG | 77% of Kenyans have electricity |
| **Vision** | Global goals the WBG wants to help reach | Share of the world's people living in poverty |

It also covers **3,158 World Bank projects** behind the results, with links to each project's page.

The data comes in yearly editions, **FY2024, FY2025 and FY2026** (the latest). The World Bank's fiscal year runs from July to June.

## How to read the answers

- **Achieved** = results *already delivered*. **Expected** = what the current projects *aim to deliver over their whole life*. Expected is usually bigger. That's normal, not a shortfall.
- Each figure says **which year's edition** it comes from and **which institution**: the whole Group (WBG), the World Bank (IBRD and IDA), IFC or MIGA.
- **Notes on the data** flag anything that affects the answer, e.g. "changes between years are indicative".
- Open **"How this was answered"** to see exactly which data was looked up and the numbers returned.
- The **✓ verified** badge means every number in the answer matches the data the assistant retrieved.
